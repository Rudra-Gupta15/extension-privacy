Welcome to VaultCraft

Everything you own, everything you want, and every price in between — organized in one local vault, not a server somewhere.

🗂️

Multi-Vault Workspaces

Split your items into Tech, Lifestyle, or fully custom vaults — keep collections separate and easy to scan.

🔗

Price Autofill

Paste a product link from Amazon, Flipkart, or any store and let VaultCraft pull the title, price, and image for you.

📅

Salary-Based Buy Planner

Turn your wishlist into a realistic month-by-month plan based on what you can actually afford.

👁️

Financial Privacy Mode

Hide your owned and planned totals instantly with one tap — handy for screen-sharing or public spaces.

💾

Backup & Restore

Export your entire vault to a single JSON file and bring it back anytime — your data, portable and yours.

🔒

100% Local Storage

Everything lives on your device in IndexedDB. No accounts, no backend, no analytics — ever.

🏺

Built local-first, on purpose

VaultCraft never phones home. The only network request it ever makes is fetching the exact product URL you paste in — nothing else, nothing automatic.

Get started in 3 steps

1

Create your first vault

Open the extension and start with the default Tech or Lifestyle vault, or make your own.

2

Add an item

Paste a product link to autofill details, or add items manually — mark them as owned or planned.

3

Build your buy plan

Set your monthly budget in the Planner tab and let VaultCraft map out when you can afford what's on your list.
