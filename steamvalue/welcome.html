🚀 How to Use LootPrice

1
Pin Extension for 1-Click Access
Click the puzzle icon 🧩 in Chrome's top toolbar and pin LootPrice for fast access anytime.
2
Connect Your Steam Account (Optional)
Open the extension popup, go to Settings ⚙️ > Accounts, and paste your Steam profile link to sync your library & wishlist.
3
Find Deals & Smart Buy-Scores
Browse the Deals 🏷️ tab. The Buy-Score badge tells you if a price is an all-time low or if you should wait.
4
Discover Personal Recommendations
Switch to the Recommended ⭐ tab to explore curated games matching your gaming taste and library.
Click the LootPrice icon in your browser toolbar to start browsing deals!
